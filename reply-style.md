# Reply style (emphasis)

These rules shape replies in this terminal. A display plugin draws `[[Word]]` pills in a shape and color per family and colors bold text; the stored message keeps plain Markdown.

- **Brief first.** A reply that reports an investigation, an analysis, a run, a change, or proposes what to do next opens with a brief: one paragraph of one to three sentences that starts with a mode pill and says what was checked, done or proposed, the outcome, and anything the user must do. The display draws it as a card. The details follow after a blank line. Skip the brief for short answers, conceptual explanations, and manuscript text.
- **Pills mark status, evidence and decisions only.** A pill is `[[Word]]` beside the thing it describes. Use exactly these words; a known word may carry short detail ([[Completed 16/16]], [[Deviation D75]]). Any other word is drawn as a dim tag.
  - **Mode.** Kind of work. Opens the brief (see below); nowhere else: [[Investigate]] (I looked into something and report what I found), [[Propose]] (I suggest what to do next), [[Changed]] (I edited files, data or jobs), [[Ran]] (I ran an analysis, test or job).
  - **Evidence.** Where a claim comes from. Mark every factual claim about data, results or code whose source is not obvious: [[Observed]] (checked on disk or in a run), [[Inferred]] (reasoned from other evidence), [[Unverified]] (not checked), [[Cited]] (taken from a paper or doc, not checked here).
  - **Verdict.** When the user states or asks you to confirm a claim, open the answer with one of these: [[Confirmed]] (the claim holds), [[Corrected]] (the claim is partly wrong), [[Contradicted]] (the claim is wrong).
  - **Gate.** Pre-registered pass/stop thresholds for an experiment and their outcomes. A miss starts an investigation of why, never a verdict on its own; Fold only after an independent review agrees: [[Gate met]] (a pre-registered gate passed), [[Gate missed]] (a gate missed; starts an investigation of why, not a verdict), [[Fold]] (direction abandoned, only after independent review), [[Shelve]] (paused for lack of resources; the project lead decides), [[Exploratory]] (no gate applies), [[Underpowered]] (a null too weak to settle anything).
  - **Status.** State of a piece of work: [[Done]] (finished), [[Passed]] (tests or checks passed), [[Handed over]] (work returned to the lead), [[Fixed]] (a defect repaired), [[Recommended]] (the option I pick), [[Failed]] (something broke), [[Blocked]] (cannot proceed), [[Partial]] (some of it done), [[Skipped]] (left out on purpose), [[At risk]] (could fail).
  - **Ask.** Something only the user can do or decide. Repeat each one in the last line of a long reply: [[Needs you]] (an action only the user can take), [[Decision]] (a call only the user makes).
  - **Flag.** Any fallback, compromise or change of plan the user was not told about beforehand. Follow it with **Logged:** and the path of the .md that records it: [[Deviation]] (an undisclosed fallback or compromise).
  - **Job.** Slurm job states; may carry the job id or counts, e.g. [[Completed 16/16]], [[OOM 52084096]]: [[Queued]] (waiting in Slurm), [[Submitted]] (sent to Slurm), [[Running]] (running), [[Cancelled]] (cancelled), [[Completed]] (exit 0), [[OOM]] (out of memory), [[Killed 143]] (SIGTERM, usually the time limit).
  - **Owner.** Who owns data, derivatives, code or config you touched or propose to touch: [[Owned]] (made by this task), [[Shared]] (lab-shared storage others use), [[Foreign]] (someone else's, such as another person's derivatives).
  - **Hazard.** Consequences, stated before the action is taken, with what is lost or spent: [[Destructive]] (deletes or overwrites), [[Irreversible]] (cannot be undone), [[GPU quota]] (spends GPU allocation).
- **Every attention pill carries its action.** After [[Unverified]], [[Gate missed]], [[Failed]], [[Blocked]], [[Needs you]], [[Decision]], [[Deviation]], a hazard or a failed job, give the one fact needed to act, introduced by a bold lead-in ending in a colon: **Check:**, **Do:**, **Next:**, **Unblock:**, **Left:**, **Run:**, **Guard:**, **Undo:**, **See:**, **Logged:**.
- **Statistics stay in words and numbers.** Report effects with their CI or adjusted p in plain text; do not make significance into a pill. [[Underpowered]] is the exception.
- **Bold the anchor terms**, one to three per paragraph: the dataset, model, cohort, decision or option the sentence turns on. Preferred bold lead labels: Question, Answer, Data, Cohort, Method, Model, Result, Effect, Evidence, Caveat, Gate, Deviation, Claim, Cause, Fix, Risk, Next, Decision, Status, Plan, Why, How.
- **Keep text that will be copied clean.** No pills, bold color labels or tree shapes in manuscript paragraphs (give those as blockquotes, drawn at full brightness), paths, commands, code, literal output, or anything written to a file.
- **Shape follows information.** `├──` and `└──` for what belongs to what, `→` for a sequence, `✓` or `✗` beside a checked item.
